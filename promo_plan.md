# Clyde Promo Video: Visual Storyboard & Animation Plan

## Creative Vision & Atmosphere
* **Mood:** Sleek, high-tech, professional, intelligent.
* **Aesthetic Theme:** "Neon Tech" - dark sleek background (`#0A0A0A`) with vibrant electric cyan (`#00F5FF`) and glowing violet (`#FF00FF`) accents representing AI and capturing real-time transcript data.
* **Core Goal:** Explain what Clyde is visually in 15-20 seconds. Live transcription -> Instant AI feedback -> Complete control.

## Scene Breakdown & Visual Layout

### Scene 1: Introduction (0s - 4s)
* **Visuals:** 
  * A pulsing digital microphone outline appears at the center of a dark void.
  * Ripples or soundwaves emanate from it as text writes out underneath: "Meet Clyde."
* **Transition:** The microphone scales up and splits into two paths, transforming into the next scene.

### Scene 2: Real-time Audio Stream (4s - 10s)
* **Visuals:**
  * Soundwaves form a clean waveform line across the screen.
  * Real-time transcript text bubbles fade in along the waveform, mimicking "You" and the "Interviewer" speaking.
  * Text writes on screen: "Your Real-Time Co-Pilot."
* **Transition:** The transcript waves converge into a central glowing, transparent intelligence node (the Clyde Ghost logo represented programmatically).

### Scene 3: Instant AI Assistant Cards (10s - 15s)
* **Visuals:**
  * From the central node, sleek frosted-glass style cards expand outward.
  * Inside the cards, structured bullet points (with a cyan accent glow) write out, detailing interview advice and live suggested questions.
  * Text underneath: "Instant AI Insights."
* **Transition:** The cards shrink back and merge together into a sleek, final clean layout.

### Scene 4: Call to Action (15s - 20s)
* **Visuals:**
  * The Clyde logo pops into the center.
  * High-contrast text appears: "Clyde. Level Up Your Live Conversations."
  * Subtitle: "Download Now at clyde.ai"
* **Outro:** Fade out to black.
